Pegasus board: fixes and clearer feedback.

* Fixed: after the opponent's capture on the Pegasus board, the app could
  stop accepting moves if the captured and the capturing piece were
  swapped quickly.
* New: if the board no longer matches the position, the app shows which
  squares differ - on screen and via LEDs - and waits until they are
  fixed before the engine or the trainer plays its next move.
* Fixed: the opening trainer no longer stalls when a line starts
  with a piece in hand.
