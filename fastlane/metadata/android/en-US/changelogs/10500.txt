Chessnut Air support, next to the DGT Pegasus.

* New: connect a Chessnut Air (board type in the connect dialog). It
  recognises every piece: captures and promotions are detected
  directly, a position set up on the board can be taken over, move
  sounds play on the board's speaker.
* Pegasus: engine-move LEDs are re-sent shortly after being shown
  (fixes guides that stayed dark).
* Pegasus: banner hints for a capture swapped too quickly and for a
  lifted piece without a legal move.
