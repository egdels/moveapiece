Chessnut Air support, next to the DGT Pegasus.

* New: connect a Chessnut Air (board type in the connect dialog). It
  knows every piece: captures and promotions are detected directly,
  a position set up on the board can be taken over, move sounds play
  on the board's speaker, a low battery is announced.
* New: the Chessnut's NEW GAME button restarts what was last played
  (same training line or same opponent) and closes any open dialog.
* Pegasus: engine-move LEDs are re-sent shortly after being shown
  (fixes guides that stayed dark); banner hints for a capture swapped
  too quickly and for a lifted piece without a legal move.
