Open it and play. MoveAPiece starts straight into a game against
Stockfish – no account, no network, no setup, no settings screen. One
"New Game" dialog covers every choice there is (opponent, colour,
strength, opening) and the app remembers it for next time.

Play against Stockfish at an adjustable strength (Elo 1320–3190),
against Maia – a human-like opponent that plays the moves a player of a
chosen rating (1100–1900) would typically play – or against another
person on the same phone.

If you own a DGT Pegasus or a Chessnut Air, Air+, Pro or Go, connect it
over Bluetooth LE and play on the real board instead of the screen. The
app works out which board it is on its own; your moves are picked up
from the board and the opponent's replies light up on its LEDs. The
screen stays usable at the same time.

Also there when you want it:

* Opening training: drill one of 20 well-known lines, the app plays the
  book side and accepts only the correct reply; a step-through library
  of the same lines
* Hint, optional live evaluation, blunder warning, post-game analysis
* Undo, redo, flip the board, PGN import and export
* Move, capture and check sounds; portrait and landscape
* English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch

Maia's nine networks are bundled and run entirely on the device via
ONNX Runtime (telemetry disabled). Everything is offline; nothing leaves
your phone. MoveAPiece is licensed under the GPLv3.
