MoveAPiece is a native Android chess app that plays against a locally
bundled Stockfish engine or against Maia, a human-like opponent based on
a bundled neural network – fully offline, without accounts, without
network access, and without proprietary services.

Optionally, MoveAPiece can connect via Bluetooth LE to a physical
DGT Pegasus chess board, so you can play against Stockfish on a real
board instead of on the screen.

Features:

* Local play: human vs. human, human vs. Stockfish, or human vs. Maia
* Maia: a human-like opponent that plays the moves a player of a chosen
  rating (1100–1900) would typically play, instead of searching for the
  strongest move. Nine pre-trained networks are bundled in the app and
  run entirely on the device via ONNX Runtime (telemetry disabled) – no
  network access
* Opening training: practice fixed lines from a built-in library of 20
  well-known openings
* Adjustable Stockfish playing strength (UCI_LimitStrength / UCI_Elo,
  1320–3190)
* Move history in algebraic notation, take back a move
* Evaluation display: optionally shows Stockfish's assessment of the
  current position (can be toggled on/off)
* PGN import/export: export games via the share dialog or load a game
  from a PGN file and continue playing
* Detection of check, checkmate, stalemate, and draws (repetition,
  50-move rule, insufficient material)
* Move, capture, and check sound effects
* Portrait and landscape orientation
* Available in German, English, French, Spanish, Italian, and Dutch
* Support for physical boards (DGT Pegasus, BLE): moves on the board
  are automatically detected and applied, and Stockfish's responses
  are shown via LEDs on the board. Full touchscreen control remains
  available at all times.
